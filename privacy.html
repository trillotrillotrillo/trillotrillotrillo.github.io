Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

"Claude Gmail read-only" is a personal tool used only by its owner, Viliam Babela, for his own Gmail accounts.

What it accesses

Read-only access to Gmail (scope gmail.readonly) for accounts the owner signs in himself. It cannot send, delete or change email.

How data is used

Email content is read on the owner's own computer only when he asks his local assistant to search or read mail. Access tokens are stored locally on that computer and are sent only to Google.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
